Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Who we are

PersonalKnowHow ("we", "us") is operated by Georgi Petkov, based in Denmark. You can reach us at privacy@personalknowhow.com.

What this policy covers

This policy covers personalknowhow.com and the hosted PersonalKnowHow service. PersonalKnowHow's source code is public on GitHub — if you fork it and run your own instance, your own deployment is not covered by this policy; you're responsible for how you handle data in your own copy.

What we collect today

Right now, PersonalKnowHow is a waitlist. The only personal data we collect is what you submit through the waitlist form on this site: your email address and an optional free-text note about what you'd use the product for. That's stored in a Cloudflare D1 database and used only to contact you if and when the hosted product opens up. We don't sell it, share it with advertisers, or use it for anything else.

The site itself sets no tracking cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. The only thing stored in your browser is a single preference — light or dark theme — in local storage, which never leaves your device.

What we collect if you use the hosted product

Today, uploading is the only way in. After you get an invite link, you upload your own LinkedIn "Request my data" export — a zip file LinkedIn generates for you under Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → Get a copy of your data. We parse the CSV files inside it to build your knowledge graph. There's no OAuth connection and no live API access to your LinkedIn account involved — it's a file you download from LinkedIn yourself and hand to us once.

More sources beyond LinkedIn may be added later. If and when that happens, this policy will be updated first with the real, specific mechanism for that source — including whether it needs your login credentials, what's actually collected, and how you can decline — before it's ever turned on for hosted customers.

This data is used only to build your personal knowledge graph and serve it back to you — through your own AI assistant, or through the tools on this site. It is never sold, never used to train models beyond generating your own embeddings, and never shared with other users or third parties beyond the infrastructure providers needed to run the service (see below).

Who processes your data

PersonalKnowHow runs entirely on Cloudflare (Workers, D1 for storage of invite/account records, R2 for your uploaded export and processed data, Workers AI for generating embeddings, Email Routing for the address above). Cloudflare acts as our infrastructure processor; we don't use any other third-party data processor.

Deletion

If you've uploaded data, there's a "Delete my data" button on your upload status page — it requires typing the email your invite was sent to as confirmation, then immediately and permanently deletes your uploaded export and processed data, and cuts off access to your MCP server right away. You can also ask us to delete your data at any time by emailing privacy@personalknowhow.com.

Security

Anything more sensitive than publicly-shareable evidence (like career interests or job application history) is kept behind bearer-token authentication, structurally separate from anything public-facing. Any public-facing export uses a fail-closed allowlist — a new category of data is excluded by default until someone deliberately decides it's safe to share, rather than being exposed automatically.

Your rights

If you're in the EU/EEA (including Denmark), you have rights under GDPR to access, correct, delete, or export your data, and to object to or restrict how it's processed. Contact us at the address above to exercise any of these.

Children

PersonalKnowHow isn't directed at, or knowingly used by, children under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
