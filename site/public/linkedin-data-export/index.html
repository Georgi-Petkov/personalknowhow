How to get your LinkedIn data export

Last updated: August 18, 2026

PersonalKnowHow needs LinkedIn's own data export (a zip file) — not your login, not a scrape. Here's how to get it.

Request the archive

  1. On LinkedIn, click your Me icon (top of the page), then Settings & Privacy.
  2. Click Data Privacy in the left menu.
  3. Under "How LinkedIn uses your data," click Download your data (sometimes shown as "Get a copy of your data").
  4. Choose "Download larger data archive, including connections, contacts, account history, and more" — the full archive, not just specific categories. This is what covers positions, education, certifications, skills, courses, endorsements, and recommendations.
  5. Click Request archive. LinkedIn will ask you to confirm your password.

You'll get two emails — wait for the second one

A first email arrives fast, often within minutes, with a smaller, partial export. A second email — the actual complete archive — arrives about a day later. This is normal, not an error.

Wait for the second email ("Your LinkedIn data archive is ready," roughly 24 hours after you requested it) and download that one. The first, partial file is missing most of what PersonalKnowHow actually uses — positions, certifications, skills, courses, endorsements, and recommendations are all much more complete in the second file. Either file will technically upload and process, but the partial one will give you a noticeably thinner result.

Download and upload

  1. Open that second email and click the download link. You have 72 hours to download it before the link expires, so don't wait too long once it arrives.
  2. Save the .zip file — don't unzip it.
  3. Upload it as-is to your personal PersonalKnowHow link.

Note: this only works from a computer, not the LinkedIn mobile app.

That's it — once uploaded, nothing further needed on your end until you hear back.
